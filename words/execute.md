# execute

## Beyond Execution

Current software merely executes. The goal is systems that understand what they are doing. Execution is the baseline, reasoning is the goal.

## Execution Models

- Von Neumann: Sequential

- Dataflow: Demand-driven

- Interaction nets: Parallel rewriting

- GPU: SIMD execution

## Performance

Execution speed matters, but correctness matters more. Fast wrong answers are useless. Slow right answers are valuable.
